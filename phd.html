PhD Scholarship in Information Theory

Department of Signal Theory and Communications, Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya, Barcelona

Applications are invited for a funded PhD scholarship at the Department of Signal Theory and Communications, Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC), Barcelona, linked to the ERC Advanced Grant on "Scaling and Concentration Laws in Information Theory" led by Professor Albert Guillén i Fàbregas. The project studies the fundamental limits of channels and sources where the optimal number of messages does not scale exponentially with the length, concentration laws for random coding, sub-optimal decoding, the design of optimal codes, as well as related statistical inference problems.

Candidate Profile

The successful candidate will have a genuine interest in Shannon Theory and a strong mathematical background. They will hold, or be about to complete, a Master's degree (or equivalent qualification giving access to doctoral studies in Spain) in Electrical Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, Physics, Computer Science or a related discipline. Prior exposure to information theory, probability, statistics, communications theory and/or channel coding is expected. A background in optimization and asymptotic methods in probability and statistics is desirable. The specific thesis topic will be determined in consultation with the PI, considering the skills and interests of the successful candidate.

The Position

The successful candidate will enrol in the UPC doctoral programme in Signal Theory and Communications. They will work closely with the PI and actively participate in the activities of the PI's research group. The project is run in collaboration with the University of Cambridge, and research stays in Cambridge will be possible.

The PhD student will be expected to:

  • carry out original research leading to a doctoral thesis;
  • contribute to ongoing research lines of the project;
  • develop numerical experiments;
  • write up results for publication in leading journals and conferences;
  • present their work at seminars and conferences, both internally and externally.

Funding for international conference travel will be available. The student may also be asked to assist in organising seminars and study groups.

Funding and Start Date

The scholarship covers tuition fees and a predoctoral contract in line with UPC salary scales for a period of 3–4 years. The position is available immediately, with some flexibility on the start date.

How to Apply

Informal enquiries are welcome and should be addressed to Professor Albert Guillén i Fàbregas at albert.guillen.fabregas@upc.edu. Interested applicants should send a CV, academic transcripts, a brief statement of research interests and the names of two referees to the same address. Applications will be considered on a rolling basis until the position is filled.
